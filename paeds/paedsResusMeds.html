Commonly Used Resuscitation Drugs for Pediatrics

Quick reference for paediatric resus drugs. Always confirm with local protocols and maximum doses.

Weight-Based Dose Calculator

Select a drug and enter the child’s weight (kg) for a rough single-dose calculation. Always cross-check with local paediatric guidelines.

Drug Dosage Route Indication
Adrenaline (Epinephrine) – arrest 0.01 mg/kg (0.1 mL/kg of 1:10 000) every 3–5 min IV, IO, ET Cardiac arrest, profound shock
Amiodarone 5 mg/kg bolus; repeat to max 15 mg/kg IV VF, pulseless VT
Atropine 0.02 mg/kg (min 0.1 mg; max 0.5 mg) IV, IO, ET Bradycardia, organophosphate poisoning
Sodium Bicarbonate 1–2 mEq/kg IV Severe metabolic acidosis, hyperkalaemia (as per protocol)
Lidocaine 1 mg/kg; repeat 0.5 mg/kg every 5–10 min (max 3 mg/kg) IV, IO, ET VF, VT (alternative to amiodarone)
Calcium gluconate 100 mg/kg (max 1 g) IV Hyperkalaemia, hypocalcaemia, calcium channel blocker toxicity
Furosemide 1 mg/kg IV, IM Pulmonary oedema, fluid overload (with careful monitoring)
Hydrocortisone 1–2 mg/kg every 6 h IV, IM Adrenal crisis, septic shock, anaphylaxis, asthma
Insulin (regular infusion) 0.1 units/kg/hour IV DKA, HHS, hyperkalaemia (with glucose)
Ipratropium 250–500 mcg every 20 min × 3 Nebulised Severe asthma, bronchiolitis (with salbutamol)
Magnesium sulfate 25–50 mg/kg over 20 min (max 2 g) IV Severe asthma, torsades de pointes, hypomagnesaemia
Dexamethasone 0.15 mg/kg (max 16 mg) IV, IM, PO Croup, asthma, anaphylaxis
Adrenaline (Epinephrine) – IM 0.01 mg/kg (max 0.3 mg) IM Anaphylaxis, refractory asthma
Salbutamol (Albuterol) 0.15 mg/kg (max 5 mg) nebulised Neb / MDI Asthma, acute wheeze, bronchiolitis
Phenobarbital 15–20 mg/kg loading dose IV Seizures, status epilepticus (after benzodiazepines)
Phenytoin (or fosphenytoin) 15–20 mg/kg loading (max rate 50 mg/min) IV Seizures, status epilepticus
