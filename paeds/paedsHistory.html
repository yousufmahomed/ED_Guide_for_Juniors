Pediatric History Taking in the ED

Quick ED Mnemonic – P-Q-R-S-T + F-A-C-E-S

This provides a rapid structure for paediatric HPI, used widely in ED teaching.

P – Provocation
What makes it better/worse?
Q – Quality
Nature of pain/problem (sharp, dull, noisy breathing, etc.)
R – Region & Radiation
Child/caregiver points to where it is.
S – Severity
Use faces scale for children.
T – Time
Onset? Sudden or gradual? Progression?
F – Feeding
Reduced intake? Poor hydration? Vomiting?
A – Activity
Lethargic? Weak? Not playing?
C – Consolability
Does the child settle with caregiver?
E – Environment
Sick contacts? Crèche exposure?
S – Sleep
Disturbed due to symptoms?

Main Complaint

Start by asking the parent/guardian about the reason for the visit...

  • Ask open-ended questions to elicit additional information
  • Use age-appropriate language and communication strategies
  • Consider cultural and social factors
Tip: One or two minutes of uninterrupted listening often gives you the whole story.
