Paediatric GCS & AVPU

Use AVPU for rapid assessment, and paediatric-modified GCS for more detailed scoring.

AVPU Scale

  • A – Alert
  • V – Responds to Voice
  • P – Responds to Pain
  • U – Unresponsive
