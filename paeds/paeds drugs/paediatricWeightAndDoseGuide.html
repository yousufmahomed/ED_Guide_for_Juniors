Paediatric Weight & Dose Guide

Use actual weight where possible. When unavailable, estimate using age-based formulas or a Broselow tape.

Weight Estimation

1–10 years: weight (kg) ≈ (age in years + 4) × 2
Infants: 3.5 kg at birth, 6 kg at 3 months, 9 kg at 1 year (approximate).
Adolescents: use clinical judgement; ask caregiver if possible.

Paracetamol dosing: 10–15 mg/kg every 4–6 hours, max 60 mg/kg/day, not exceeding adult maximum.

Example ED Drugs (Common Ranges)

  • Ibuprofen: 5–10 mg/kg PO every 6–8 hours (avoid if dehydrated or renal disease).
  • Morphine (IV): 0.05–0.1 mg/kg IV slowly, titrate to effect.
  • Ketamine (procedural sedation): typical range 1–2 mg/kg IV or 4–5 mg/kg IM (follow local protocol).
  • Salbutamol neb: 2.5 mg (< 20 kg) or 5 mg (≥ 20 kg) every 20 minutes initially in acute asthma.
Always confirm doses with your local paediatric protocol or a paediatric drug reference before prescribing.
