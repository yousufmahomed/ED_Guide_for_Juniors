Paediatric Fluids & Electrolytes

Use weight-based calculations and reassess frequently. Avoid over- or under-hydration.

Maintenance Fluids (Classic 4-2-1 Rule)

Hourly maintenance (mL/hr) =
4 mL/kg for first 10 kg
+ 2 mL/kg for next 10 kg
+ 1 mL/kg for each kg > 20 kg

Adjust according to clinical state, serum electrolytes, and local guidelines.

Dehydration

  • Mild: oral rehydration.
  • Moderate: oral or NG where possible; IV if not tolerated.
  • Severe / shock: treat as shock (10–20 mL/kg bolus) and reassess.

Electrolyte Corrections

  • Correct sodium and potassium slowly unless life-threatening derangement.
  • Use local protocols for hypernatraemia, hyponatraemia, and potassium replacement.
