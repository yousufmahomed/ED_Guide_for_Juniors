Commonly Used Antibiotics in Hospitals for Pediatric Patients

Overview

This table provides a quick reference to commonly used antibiotics and typical paediatric weight-based doses. Always check local hospital guidelines, formularies and maximum daily doses before prescribing.

Safety note: Doses here are general suggestions only. Adjust for age, renal function, infection severity, local resistance patterns and specific indications.
