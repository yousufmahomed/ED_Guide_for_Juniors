Paediatric Vaccination Schedule

Quick reference only – always confirm with the Road-to-Health booklet and the latest South African EPI schedule.
Practical ED Points:
  • Check the Road-to-Health booklet for vaccines given, missed doses, and any catch-up schedule.
  • For unvaccinated or incompletely vaccinated children, follow the national catch-up schedule.
  • In acutely unwell children, vaccination is usually deferred until stable – except in post-exposure scenarios (e.g. rabies, tetanus prophylaxis as per protocol).
  • Ask specifically about prematurity, HIV exposure/infection, and chronic illness – these may alter vaccine timing or type.
