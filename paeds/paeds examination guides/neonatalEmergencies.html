Neonatal Emergencies

Neonates (first 28 days of life) can deteriorate rapidly. Any sick neonate is an emergency until proven otherwise.

Common Presentations

  • Poor feeding, lethargy.
  • Hypothermia or fever.
  • Respiratory distress.
  • Jaundice, vomiting, abdominal distension.
  • Seizures, abnormal movements.

Initial Approach

  • ABC stabilisation (warm, dry environment, oxygen if needed).
  • Check glucose early; treat hypoglycaemia promptly.
  • IV/IO access; consider umbilical venous line in some settings.
  • Consider sepsis in any unwell neonate: start antibiotics per local protocol after cultures if possible.

Red Flags

  • Apnoea, gasping, cyanosis.
  • Poor perfusion, mottled skin, prolonged capillary refill.
  • Bulging fontanelle, seizures, reduced level of consciousness.
These require immediate senior assistance, possible NICU transfer.
